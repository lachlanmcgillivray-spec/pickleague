Bulk import a schedule

Paste one or more weeks at once instead of adding matchups by hand. Format:

Week 5
Buccaneers @ Cowboys | Thu Oct 8 8:15 PM
Packers @ Falcons | Sun Oct 11 8:20 PM
Bills @ Rams | Mon Oct 12 8:15 PM

Week 6
Seahawks @ Broncos | Oct 15 8:15 PM
...

Each "Away @ Home" line becomes a matchup. Adding | date and time after it is optional and sets that game's kickoff (which auto-locks it). Use your own time zone and include AM or PM, like Oct 11 1:00 PM, 10/11 1:00 PM or 2026-10-11 13:00. Get matchups and times from nfl.com/schedules.

Importing a week that already exists adds any new matchups without deleting anything. If a game is already there, its kickoff time gets filled in or updated, so you can paste times in later.
